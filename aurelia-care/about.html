About Aurelia Care

A hospital built on one idea: be the kind of place you would take your own family.

Our story

From 40 beds to a full hospital

Aurelia started in 2008 as a small hospital with a simple rule: explain everything, charge fairly and never turn away an emergency.

Today we are 250 beds, eight departments and more than 60 specialists, and the rule has not changed. Every new service starts with one question: would we want this for our own family?

2008

Aurelia opens with 40 beds

A small family-run hospital in Kodambakkam, with five doctors and one operation theatre.

2012

The first ICU

A 10-bed intensive care unit opens, and the emergency room starts working 24 hours.

2016

Heart and bone centres

Cardiology and Orthopaedics grow into full departments with their own wards.

2020

Care through the pandemic

Teams work around the clock, and video consults become part of everyday care.

2023

Mother and child wing

A new wing opens for maternity, newborn care and paediatrics.

2026

250 beds and counting

Eight departments, a 40-bed ICU and online booking for every patient.

What we stand for

Four promises

01

Explain first

Doctors explain the problem, the options and the cost before any treatment starts.

02

Charge fairly

Clear price lists, one itemised bill and no surprise charges at discharge.

03

Safety always

Checklists for every surgery, a hand-hygiene audit each week and a patient-safety officer on every shift.

04

Respect everyone

Every patient gets the same attention, in the ward or the suite.
