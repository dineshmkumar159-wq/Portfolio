Before you come

Good to know

Do I need to fast?
Yes. Please fast for 10 to 12 hours before your visit. You may drink plain water. Take regular medicines unless your doctor says otherwise.
How long does it take?
Most packages take 2 to 3 hours. The Executive package takes about half a day and includes breakfast.
When do I get the results?
Blood reports are ready the same day. Imaging reports are ready within 24 hours. Everything is sent to your phone and discussed with a doctor.
Can I add a test?
Yes. Tell the desk when you book, and we will add the test and show the new price before you pay.
