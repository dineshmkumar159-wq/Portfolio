Questions

Insurance, billing and records

Do you offer cashless treatment?
Yes, with most major insurers and TPAs. Bring your card at admission and our insurance desk sends the pre-authorisation request while you settle in. (Sample answer.)
What if my claim is not approved?
We tell you before treatment starts if there is a gap, and show the amount you would pay. You can choose to proceed or ask for another option.
Can I get a copy of my records?
Yes. Ask at the medical records desk with your ID. Reports are ready within two working days, and recent ones are also sent to your phone.
Is parking available?
Free parking for patients and visitors in the basement and the rear lot. Ambulances use the dedicated emergency entrance on the east side.
Can family stay overnight?
One attendant can stay with each patient. Private rooms and suites have a bed for them. In wards we provide a reclining chair.
