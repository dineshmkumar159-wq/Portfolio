The hospital's WhatsApp assistant

This is how a patient would chat with Aurelia Care Hospital on WhatsApp. Type a question or tap a button in the phone.

Try:

  1. The patient messages the hospital's WhatsApp number.
  2. WhatsApp passes the message to the hospital's server.
  3. The server finds the answer and sends the reply, with quick-reply buttons.

Demo only. This page runs in your browser and is not connected to real WhatsApp. The real version runs on a Node.js server through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Aurelia Care Hospital is an imaginary hospital, and every doctor, price and number here is a sample.
