How we roast

From green bean to your door in four steps

Every lot is sampled, profiled and roasted in batches of 12 kg or less.

01

Source and sample

We cup every lot at the estate and buy only what scores 84 or higher on the SCA scale.

02

Profile the roast

Each origin gets its own curve, tracked by temperature and time, from first crack to cooling.

03

Rest, then pack

Beans rest for 48 hours so the carbon dioxide settles, then go into one-way valve bags.

04

Ship on your day

Roasted to order and delivered within 48 hours in Chennai and 4 days across India.

FAQ

Good questions

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

Roasted to order. In Chennai it arrives 48 hours after roasting, and across India within 4 days. The roast date is printed on every bag.

Can I skip, pause or cancel?

Yes. Skip a delivery or pause up to 3 months from your account, and cancel at any time with no fee.

Which grind should I choose?

Espresso for machines, filter for pour-over and drip, French press for immersion. If you are not sure, whole bean keeps the coffee freshest for longer.

Do you deliver outside Chennai?

We ship across India. Orders under ₹999 have a delivery fee of ₹60, and orders from ₹999 are free.
