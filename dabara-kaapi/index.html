How we brew

Three steps, none of them rushed

Every tumbler goes through the same order, every morning, before the doors open.

  1. Decoction

    Freshly ground peaberry beans and chicory drip through a steel filter at 1 : 3. We start at 2:30 am so the first batch is ready by opening.

  2. Milk

    Full-cream milk is boiled twice and held hot. Sugar goes in last, a level teaspoon per tumbler unless you ask for less.

  3. The pull

    Decoction and milk are poured between tumbler and dabara from a height until the froth rises. It cools it to drinking temperature, too.
