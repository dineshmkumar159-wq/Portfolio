Anatomy of a parfum

Three layers, unfolding over the day

A parfum is built in three stages. The first impression fades in minutes, the heart settles within the hour, and the base stays on the skin until evening.

Top · first 15 minutes

Bergamot, cardamom, sea salt

Bright and light, the part you smell first.

Heart · 30 minutes to 3 hours

Jasmine sambac, rose, saffron

The character of the scent.

Base · 3 hours and beyond

Oud, vetiver, amber, musk

Warm, slow and long lasting.

Ritual

Apply it slowly

Step one

Skin first

Apply after a shower on clean, lightly moisturised skin so the parfum holds longer.

Step two

Pulse points

One spray on each wrist and one at the base of the neck. Do not rub, it bruises the top notes.

Step three

Let it open

Wait ten minutes. The heart notes arrive and the scent becomes yours.
