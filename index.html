FAQ

Questions clients ask

How long does a website take?

A one-page site usually takes about a week. Larger sites take two to three weeks, depending on how quickly I get your content and photos.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, contact details, a few photos and a list of what you offer. If you don't have the text ready, I can help you write it.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. I design for phones first, because many of your customers will open the site there.

Who owns the website?

You do. When the project is finished, I hand over the files and help you put them online.

Can you help with a domain and hosting?

Yes. I can help you choose a domain name and put the site online on free hosting.

Do the apps take real payments?

The demo apps use test payments only. For a real business I connect a payment service such as Razorpay, and orders are confirmed only after the payment is verified.

Need a website for your business?

Send me a message with a few details about your business and I'll reply with ideas and a simple quote.
