About us

A school that started with ninety children.

Twenty-eight years, five campuses and one steady idea: notice every child.

Our story

One idea, grown five times.

Meridian began in a rented bungalow in Adyar in 1998. Each new campus was opened because families asked for one nearby, and each took on the personality of its neighbourhood while keeping the same classroom culture.

1998

Adyar campus opens with 90 children and 9 teachers.

2004

Anna Nagar campus opens with a 400-seat auditorium.

2010

Velachery campus opens with the first robotics lab.

2013

Tambaram campus opens on eleven acres of fields and orchard.

2018

Porur campus opens, offering Cambridge IGCSE and A Level.

2026

Over 9,400 learners across five campuses, one shared standard.
