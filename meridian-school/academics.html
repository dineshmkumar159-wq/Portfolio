Academics

Learning that grows with your child.

Five stages, one shared approach: understand first, then practise, then apply.

Choose a stage

What your child will study.

Pre-KG to UKG · ages 3 to 6

Foundation

Learning through play, stories and real things.

How we assess: Observation notes and a termly portfolio. No exams.

What children study

  • Language and early reading
  • Number sense with objects
  • Art, music and movement
  • Sand, water and garden play
  • Social skills and sharing
Classes I to V · ages 6 to 11

Primary

Solid reading, number sense and a love of asking why.

How we assess: Class tests, projects and a termly learning conference with parents.

What children study

  • English, Tamil and Hindi
  • Mathematics and environmental science
  • Art, music and dance
  • Library hour every week
  • Swimming and games
Classes VI to VIII · ages 11 to 14

Middle

Projects, labs and the first taste of independence.

How we assess: Unit tests, project exhibitions and a yearly portfolio review.

What children study

  • Sciences taught in labs
  • Mathematics and computational thinking
  • Social science through projects
  • Languages and literature
  • Clubs and electives
Classes IX to X · ages 14 to 16

Secondary

Board preparation without losing curiosity.

How we assess: Periodic tests, mock boards and one-to-one study plans.

What children study

  • CBSE Class X board syllabus (IGCSE at Porur)
  • Practical sciences and maths
  • Career exploration and counselling
  • Study-skills and exam-technique workshops
  • Service hours and leadership roles
Classes XI to XII · ages 16 to 18

Senior

Streams, mentors and university guidance.

How we assess: Board and entrance preparation with fortnightly mentor meetings. Offered at Adyar, Anna Nagar, Velachery and Porur.

What children study

  • Science (PCM, PCB), Commerce and Humanities
  • Computer science and applied maths
  • Cambridge A Level at Porur
  • University and entrance-exam counselling
  • Internships and research projects
Curriculum

CBSE or Cambridge: how to choose.

CBSE

Adyar · Anna Nagar · Velachery · Tambaram

The national curriculum, taught through projects and labs. Best if you plan for Indian entrance exams and want the widest choice of campus.

Cambridge

Porur

IGCSE at Classes IX to X and A Level at XI to XII, with small classes. Best if you plan for universities abroad or want a more flexible subject mix.
