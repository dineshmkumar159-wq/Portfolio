FAQ

Quick answers.

What age can my child start Pre-KG?

Children who are three years old by 31 May of the joining year can start Pre-KG.

Can I apply to more than one campus?

Yes. Use one form and mention a second choice. We will tell you where seats are open.

Can my child move between campuses later?

Yes, subject to seats. Curriculum is common across the four CBSE campuses. Porur follows Cambridge.

Is school transport compulsory?

No. It is optional and priced by distance. Routes cover 30+ localities.

Which documents do we need?

Birth certificate, address proof, passport photos, previous report card (from Class II) and a transfer certificate (from Class II).
