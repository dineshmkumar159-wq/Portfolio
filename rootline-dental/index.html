Your first visit

What happens in the chair

  1. We talk first

    About 10 minutes on your history, what worries you, and any past treatment.

  2. We examine

    A full check of teeth and gums, with an X-ray only if it will change the plan.

  3. You get a plan

    A written plan with each step and its cost. Nothing starts until you say yes.

Questions

Before you come in

Does a root canal hurt?

The tooth is numbed first, so most people feel pressure and not pain. A day or two of soreness afterwards is common. Tell us straight away if anything feels sharp during treatment.

How often should I have a check-up?

Every six months suits most adults. If you have gum disease or a lot of fillings, your dentist may ask to see you more often.

Can I bring my child?

Yes. Dr. Iyer sees children from age 3, and a first visit can be as simple as sitting in the chair and counting teeth.

Do you take insurance?

Ask when you book. We can give you an itemised bill to submit to your insurer.
